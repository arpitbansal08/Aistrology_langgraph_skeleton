# General Astrology Skill

## Purpose

Answer broad natal-chart, personality, life-theme, timing, and chart-explanation questions. Keep interpretations reflective and practical, not deterministic.

## Required inputs

Before calculating a natal chart, collect or confirm:

- Birth date in an unambiguous format (for example, 8 February 2002).
- Birth time, including AM/PM or a 24-hour time, and whether it is exact or estimated.
- Birthplace (city, state/province, country).
- The astrology system when the user has a preference: tropical or sidereal, house system, and zodiac/ayanamsha. Default only when the application has a documented standard.

For a transit, return, progression, or electional question, also collect the relevant date, time, and location. Explain that an unknown birth time prevents a reliable Ascendant, houses, angles, and many timing techniques.

## Tools and calculations

Use a trusted ephemeris or chart-calculation tool for all chart data. The tool workflow is:

1. Geocode the birthplace and obtain latitude, longitude, and historical time-zone offset.
2. Convert local birth time to UTC, accounting for daylight-saving or historical time-zone rules.
3. Calculate planetary longitudes, Ascendant, Midheaven, house cusps, lunar nodes, and aspects for the selected system.
4. For timing questions, calculate the requested transit, progression, solar/lunar return, or dasha only after the natal chart is established.

Never invent placements, aspects, house positions, dates, or calculations. If a calculator is unavailable, ask for a chart export or offer a qualitative explanation without claiming chart-specific results.

## Response guidance

- State the chart system and birth-data confidence used.
- Distinguish calculated facts from interpretive possibilities.
- Explain the most relevant placements, houses, rulers, and aspects in plain language.
- Treat astrology as reflective or entertainment-oriented guidance; preserve the user’s agency.
- Avoid absolute predictions, guarantees, fear-based claims, or instructions that replace professional advice.
- For health, legal, financial, or safety-sensitive matters, give a brief appropriate disclaimer and encourage qualified support.
