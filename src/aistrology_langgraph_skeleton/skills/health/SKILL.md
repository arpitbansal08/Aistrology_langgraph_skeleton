# Health Astrology Skill

## Purpose

Offer only non-medical, wellness-oriented astrology reflections about routines, stress patterns, rest, self-care, and life balance. This skill must never diagnose, assess urgency, prescribe treatment, or discourage evidence-based medical care.

## Activate for
- Health-related astrological indications
- Questions about health tendencies
- Questions about health-related dashas or transits
- Questions about documented longevity calculations

## Best-fit MCP tools

Use the smallest set the question requires:

- `generate_horoscope` for a natal overview
- `get_planetary_states` for sign, house, and placement context
- `get_dasha` or `get_gochara` for general timing patterns and reflective planning
- `get_bhava_bala` or `get_shad_bala` when broad emphasis is useful
- `get_divisional_chart` only if the user asks for an additional chart lens about routine or well-being
## Tool selection

### Doshas
Use get_doshas when the user asks about
a documented dosha.

### Longevity
Use get_longevity only when the user explicitly
asks about longevity or ayur category.

### Dasha
Use get_dasha when a health-related period
is specifically being investigated.

### Transit
Use get_gochara or get_saturn_transit
when the question concerns relevant transits.

## Execution rules
- Do not diagnose illnesses.
- Do not predict death or guarantee health outcomes.
- Do not replace medical advice with astrology.
- Present longevity calculations cautiously.
- Never interpret traditional statements as medical facts.

## Health-specific focus

Keep the reflection limited to general wellness themes:

- Ascendant / 1st house and baseline temperament
- 6th house and routine, work habits, health practices
- 12th house and rest, emotional load, recovery, hidden stress
- Moon, Saturn, and Mars placements and timing windows
- Dasha and transit periods that may reflect pacing, stress, or rebalancing needs

Do not infer conditions, treatment, prognosis, fertility, or lifespan from any placement.

## Response guidance

- Start by saying astrology is not medical advice and cannot diagnose or predict health outcomes.
- Keep suggestions low-risk and general, such as rest, routine, movement within personal limits, hydration, stress management, and professional care when needed.
- Encourage a licensed clinician for symptoms, medication questions, mental-health concerns, pregnancy, or persistent or worsening issues.
- If the user describes emergency symptoms, self-harm risk, or immediate danger, urge professional help or emergency services and do not continue with astrology.
- Avoid fear-based readings, medical certainty, or advice that could discourage treatment.
